# Every email this kit can send

_Generated by `scripts/render-emails-02.js`. Do not edit by hand; change the copy
in `src/core/copy.js` and regenerate. Rendered against a sample practice on 2099-06-16._

Read this before installing. It is the whole of what a client will ever receive.
The emails remind and ask. They state no penalty, rate or figure, and never say
that anything has been submitted or paid. The kit never submits a VAT return to HMRC.
In these samples a period that ends on the last day of a month has its deadline calculated by the kit; any other period is shown with the due date typed into the HMRC Due Date column, because the kit does not calculate those.

## Advance notice, 21 days out

The first checklist. Calm, no pressure, asks the client to work through the list and send what is outstanding.

```text
To:      ap@boroughcafe.co.uk
Subject: Advance notice: VAT return due Tuesday 7 July 2099

Dear Borough Cafe Ltd,

This is an early reminder of your upcoming VAT return deadline:

  Period ended 31 May 2099: return due Tuesday 7 July 2099 (21 days to go)

Any VAT payment is due by the same date, so please allow time for it to reach HMRC. If you pay by Direct Debit, HMRC collects it 3 working days after the deadline, but the return itself must still be in by the date above.

To get your return ready in time, please check that:
  - Every sales and purchase invoice for the period, and any credit notes, is recorded in your accounting software.
  - Your bank, card and cash accounts are reconciled up to the end of the period.
  - Any adjustments, such as corrections to earlier returns, are recorded.
  - If you use more than one program or spreadsheet, figures pass between them digitally and are not retyped by hand.
  - Anything we have asked you for, such as missing invoices or receipts, has been sent to us.

Please work through the checklist, and send us anything that is still outstanding when you can.

Once everything is in place, let us know and we will prepare the return for your approval.
If you have already told us everything is ready, thank you, and please ignore this message.

Kind regards,
Sam Patel
```
