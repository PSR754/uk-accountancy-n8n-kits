# Every email this kit can send

_Generated by `scripts/render-emails-01.js`. Do not edit by hand; change the copy
in `src/core/copy.js` and regenerate. Rendered against a sample practice on 2099-06-16._

Read this before installing. It is the whole of what a client will ever receive.
The emails remind and ask. They state no penalty, rate or figure, and never say
that anything has been filed or paid.

## Advance notice, 30 days out

The first reminder. Calm, no pressure, asks the client to start gathering what is needed.

```text
To:      ap@boroughcafe.co.uk
Subject: Advance notice: Example filing deadline Thursday 16 July 2099

Dear Borough Cafe Ltd,

This is an early reminder of an upcoming deadline:

  Example filing — Thursday 16 July 2099 (30 days to go)

Please start gathering any records or information we still need from you, and send them over when you can.

If you have already sent us everything we need, thank you, and please ignore this message.

Kind regards,
Sam Patel
Example Accountants Ltd

—
Example Accountants Ltd
If anything here looks wrong, please call us on 01234 567890 before replying.
We will never ask you to change the bank details you pay us into by email. If you receive such a request, telephone us on a number you already hold before acting on it.
```

## A client with several deadlines

One email, every deadline that is due a reminder listed, pitched at the tone the nearest one warrants. The client is not sent three separate emails.

```text
To:      ap@multi.co.uk
Subject: Urgent reminder: 2 deadlines, the first Tuesday 23 June 2099

Dear Multi Ltd,

These deadlines are now close:

  Example filing A — Tuesday 23 June 2099 (7 days to go)
```
